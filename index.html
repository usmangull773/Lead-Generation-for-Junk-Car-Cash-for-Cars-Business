Growth plan

From launch to predictable cost-per-car

A focused first 30 days to build tracking you can trust and get real cars coming in, then a clear path to scale. This is the plan we'd refine together on our first call.

First 30 DaysBuild & launch (detailed)
Days 31–60Optimize & scale
Days 61–90Expand & systematize
Week 1Foundation & tracking
  • Full audit of current setup, Google Business Profile and any existing ads to find what's leaking money.
  • Tracking that tells the truth: GA4 + Google Tag Manager, and call tracking (CallRail-style) so every phone lead is recorded and attributed.
  • Conversion actions defined for calls, form submits and texts, plus the pipeline to import "car purchased" back into Google Ads as an offline conversion.
  • Landing page (like the demo here) live with click-to-call, instant-quote form and same-day pickup messaging.
Week 2Launch
  • Search campaigns live on high-intent keywords: "sell my junk car", "cash for junk cars near me", "junk car removal".
  • Tight geo-targeting around Chino, Chino Hills, Ontario & nearby ZIPs, so no spend is wasted outside your tow radius.
  • Call-focused ad copy + call assets so mobile searchers can tap to call instantly.
  • Negative keyword list from day one (parts, repair, free, DMV, etc.) to block junk clicks.
Week 3Qualify & tune
  • Listen to real call recordings to separate true sellers from tire-kickers and refine keywords accordingly.
  • Search-term cleanup: add negatives daily, shift budget toward terms producing calls.
  • Mobile & schedule bid adjustments toward the hours/devices that convert.
  • Google Business Profile optimized for "near me" and map leads: categories, photos, review prompts.
Week 4First optimization cycle
  • Pause the wasters, scale the winners based on cost-per-purchased-car, not clicks.
  • Landing page A/B test started (headline / form length) to lift conversion rate.
  • First clear report: spend → leads → calls → cars bought → cost per car, in the dashboard you saw.
  • Review call together to lock the plan for scaling in month 2.
🎯 End of 30 days: trustworthy tracking, live campaigns bringing qualified calls, and a dashboard that tells you exactly which ad dollars became purchased cars.
Days 31–60 · Optimize & scale

Turn winners into volume

  • Scale budget into the proven low-cost-per-car keywords and dayparts.
  • Add call-only campaigns and test Performance Max with a strong lead feed.
  • Roll out the winning landing-page variant and add trust signals that lifted conversions.
  • Drive Google review velocity to strengthen Map Pack & organic "near me" leads.
  • Tighten lead-quality feedback loop so bidding optimizes toward real purchased cars.
Days 61–90 · Expand & systematize

Predictable, repeatable growth

  • Expand geo into profitable neighboring cities once cost-per-car is stable.
  • Layer in retargeting and, where it fits, Local Services Ads for extra call volume.
  • Standardize weekly reporting & a simple forecast: "$X spend ≈ Y cars."
  • Build a small library of tested ads & landing pages for seasonal pushes.
  • Shift focus from "more leads" to lower cost per purchased car at higher volume.
